\section{Origin: Each Failure Forced a Component}\label{sec:timeline}
\sys{} began as a learning exercise. Its builder first evaluated a widely used multi-agent framework, judged it unsuitable for consistent and safe use, and deliberately chose a lean, deterministic dispatcher instead of a swarm (decision log, 21 June). The architecture was then grown one failure at a time. Table~\ref{tab:timeline} lists every failure for which we found evidence in the system's own decision log, audit log, middleware-correction log, design documents or version history. Each row names the component that the failure forced into existence. Several components are independent re-discoveries of known patterns (Section~\ref{sec:related}); we cite those patterns rather than claim them.
